Before you start

Do I need an account?

No. Open a game and press Start. Saved games and some scores stay in this browser; they do not sync across devices.

Can I play on a phone?

Most games support taps, swipes or on-screen controls. Space Shooter requires a keyboard. For larger boards, open the game separately or turn your device sideways. Check each control guide.

Is there a global leaderboard?

There are no online accounts or shared leaderboards here. Personal device records are clearly labeled and are never presented as global rankings.
