How to play

Updated 2026-09-21

About this game

Take five penalty shots. Choose a target for each kick while the goalkeeper randomly dives left, right, or stays central.

How to play

Start and select a point inside the goal. Wait for the shot and result animation before taking the next kick.

Controls

Click or tap the goal area. There is no power charging; your selected point sets the aim.

Scoring and ending

Goals and misses are counted across five rounds. Saves mainly depend on the final horizontal distance between the ball and goalkeeper.

Practical tips

Try both sides and vary your aim. The keeper is random, so repeating one target does not guarantee a goal.
