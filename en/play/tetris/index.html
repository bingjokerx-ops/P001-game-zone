About this game

Arrange falling shapes into complete rows. The drop speed increases as you clear lines, making the next-piece preview useful for planning.

How to play

Move and rotate each piece before it lands. A completely filled horizontal row disappears. Avoid leaving buried gaps.

Controls

←/→ move, ↑ rotates, ↓ drops faster, and Space pauses or resumes. On a phone, use the on-screen movement and rotation buttons.

Scoring and ending

Clearing 1/2/3/4 lines at once earns 100/300/500/800 points multiplied by the current level. Every 10 lines raises the level. The game ends when a new piece cannot fit.

Practical tips

Keep the stack relatively flat. Save narrow gaps for long pieces, but do not build too high while waiting for one.
