How to play

Updated 2026-09-21

About this game

Wait for green, then click as quickly as possible to measure your visual response time. The random wait prevents reliable timing guesses.

How to play

Click the test area to get ready. Wait until it turns green, then respond. Click the result area to begin another trial.

Controls

Click with the mouse or tap the test area. Each trial needs one click to prepare and another to respond.

Scoring and ending

Results are milliseconds: lower is faster. Clicking early records “Too early” and is excluded from valid attempts and averages. Statistics last for the current page.

Practical tips

Position your pointer or finger first and watch the color signal. Compare several trials and the average rather than judging one unusually fast result.
