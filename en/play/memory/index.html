How to play

Updated 2026-09-21

About this game

Find eight matching pairs among sixteen face-down cards. Remembering both the picture and its position is more useful than guessing.

How to play

Turn over two cards at a time. Matching cards stay open; different cards turn back after about a second. Find all eight pairs to finish.

Controls

Click or tap a card. Wait for a mismatched pair to turn back before selecting another card.

Scoring and ending

Each pair of flips counts as one attempt. The timer starts with the first card. Finish in fewer attempts and less time.

Practical tips

Explore one row or region at a time. When a familiar picture appears, look for its known partner before opening another new card.
