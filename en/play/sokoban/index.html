How to play

Updated 2026-09-21

About this game

Push crates onto marked targets in a warehouse. Crates can be pushed but never pulled, so a wrong move can trap one.

How to play

Walk toward a crate to push it one square. A wall or another crate blocks the push. Cover every target with a crate to complete the layout.

Controls

Use arrow keys, lowercase WASD, or the on-screen arrows. Select a level with its button; Reset restores its starting layout.

Scoring and ending

Moves and pushes are counted separately. Complete a layout to advance. There are five practice levels. Undo reverses one move; Reset starts the level again.

Practical tips

Plan which side of each crate you must stand on. Avoid corners without targets and keep a route around the crates.
