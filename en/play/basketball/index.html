How to play

Updated 2026-09-21

About this game

Score baskets within 45 seconds. Consecutive makes increase the value of each shot, while drag direction and distance determine the launch.

How to play

Start, drag opposite to the intended shot, and release. The hoop is upper-right, so usually pull down-left. Wait until the current ball is gone before shooting again.

Controls

Hold and drag with the mouse or a finger, then release. A longer drag adds power up to a fixed cap.

Scoring and ending

The first consecutive basket earns 1 point, the second and third earn 2 each, and the fourth onward earns 3 each. A miss resets the streak. Time expires after 45 seconds.

Practical tips

Adjust the angle a little at a time, then tune power. Watch whether the shot falls short or passes above or below the hoop.
