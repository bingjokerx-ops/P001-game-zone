How to play

Updated 2026-09-21

About this game

Switch between three lanes to avoid traffic. Your car advances and speeds up automatically, leaving you to choose a safe route.

How to play

Start and change lanes before another car gets close. Lane changes animate gradually, so react early.

Controls

Use ←/→, lowercase A/D, or the on-screen left and right buttons to move one lane at a time.

Scoring and ending

Score rises while you drive, and speed increases with progress. A collision ends the run. Your best score is saved locally.

Practical tips

Check the spacing between cars and confirm the destination lane is clear before moving. Keep another escape route available.
