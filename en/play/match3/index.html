How to play

Updated 2026-09-21

About this game

Swap adjacent gems on an 8×8 board. You have thirty successful swaps, so falling gems and chain reactions can make each move worth more.

How to play

Select a gem, then an orthogonally adjacent gem. A row or column of at least three equal gems clears; replacements can trigger further matches.

Controls

Click or tap two neighboring gems. Diagonal swaps are not allowed. Wait for clearing and falling animations to finish.

Scoring and ending

Each cleared gem earns 10 points. Only a swap that creates a match uses a move; invalid swaps revert for free. The game ends after 30 moves.

Practical tips

Look for swaps low on the board to disturb more gems above. Check whether one swap can create both a horizontal and a vertical match.
