How to play

Updated 2026-09-21

About this game

Find the trophy in a randomly generated maze. Visited paths remain marked, and later levels gradually grow larger.

How to play

Travel from the upper-left start to the lower-right trophy through open passages. Backtrack from dead ends and explore another branch.

Controls

Use arrow keys, lowercase WASD, or on-screen arrows. New Maze regenerates the current level; Next Level follows a successful escape.

Scoring and ending

The game tracks moves and elapsed time. Walking into a wall adds no move. Reaching the trophy completes the level, with no countdown limit.

Practical tips

Remember unexplored branches at junctions. Use the visited trail to avoid repeating a dead end.
