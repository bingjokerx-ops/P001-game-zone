About this game

Identify the actual ink color within 30 seconds while ignoring the color word’s meaning. This is an attention and response game.

How to play

Read the font color, then select its matching button. If the word “red” is drawn in blue, choose blue. Every answer changes the question and shuffles buttons.

Controls

Click or tap a color button. Match the lettering’s color, not the word meaning or background.

Scoring and ending

A correct answer earns 10 plus 2 × the previous correct streak. A wrong answer resets the streak without removing earned points. Results appear after 30 seconds.

Practical tips

Focus on color rather than reading. Button positions change every round, so do not rely on a fixed location.
