How to play

Updated 2026-09-21

About this game

Launch a ball into bumpers and top targets, then catch it with the bottom paddle. This version uses one horizontally moving paddle.

How to play

Start, launch, and keep the ball above the bottom edge. After a lost ball, launch the replacement again.

Controls

Move the mouse or drag sideways to position the paddle. Click the canvas or press Space to launch.

Scoring and ending

You have three balls. Bumpers earn 50 points. Each top target awards 300, 200, then 100 on its first three hits. Losing all balls ends the round.

Practical tips

Get beneath a falling ball early. Off-center paddle hits change its sideways speed, and you can position the paddle before the next launch.
